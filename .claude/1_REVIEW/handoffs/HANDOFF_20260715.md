## HPWL reporting semantics (just changed — important)
- `Best GP HPWL` = our GP HPWL, MASKED (nets>ignore_net_degree=100 dropped) — pairs with XPlace's
  masked GP metric. `XPlace GP HPWL` = XPlace masked_hpwl at GP Stop (GP-only), ISPD2005 only.
  `Ratio` = Best GP / XPlace GP = honest GP-vs-GP.
- `Final HPWL Exact` (new) = final HPWL over ALL nets (unmasked) — matches XPlace's post-GP exact/
  published convention. Use `computeTotalWirelength(method, 1e9)` for all-nets (NOT -1 = excludes all).
- To populate mgc/bigblue3-4 XPlace GP refs: run XPlace locally (recipe in `grid_sizing_and_target_density`
  / `xplace_build_and_run`), read `GP Stop! masked_hpwl`, and for mgc multiply by site_width (raw-DBU
  frame) before putting in Output.cpp's `xplace_hpwl` map.
