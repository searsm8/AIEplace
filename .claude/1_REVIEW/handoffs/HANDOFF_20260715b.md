## Overflow / filler reconciliation — MEASURED and REFUTED
XPlace's GP STOP overflow (`overflow_fn`, run_placement_nesterov.py:41) uses `movable + FILLER` density;
our convergence overflow (`computeOverflow(true)`, Density.cpp:426) excludes fillers = XPlace's EXACT
overflow (`get_obj_overflow`). Clamps are byte-identical (both √2·bin, area-conserving expand_ratio).
Hypothesis was: include fillers to match XPlace's lower stop → let bigblue3 converge.
**Measured on converged adaptec1** (4-way diagnostic): clamp/no-filler 0.057, sharp/no-filler 0.136,
**clamp/+filler 0.081**, sharp/+filler 0.209. Including fillers *RAISES* overflow → would REGRESS
adaptec1 past its 0.07 stop. Our clamp/no-filler (0.057) already ≈ XPlace stop (0.042). **Do not include
fillers in the convergence signal.** bigblue3's high floor (0.107 vs adaptec1's 0.057, same clamp rule)
points to a grid/clamp-strength effect at the 2048 auto-grid (tiny bins → √2·bin barely inflates cells
→ clamped≈sharp). See memory `overflow_filler_reconciliation`.

## ⭐ Die-area investigation (PRIORITY — root cause to identify)
**Symptom (adaptec1):** sw_only die **1.245e8** vs XPlace **1.142e8** (+9%); sw_only fixed **6.41e7 (51%)**
vs XPlace **4.916e7 (43%)** (+30%). Movable area matches exactly (3.729e7). This feeds the density-target
denominator, filler count, and overflow normalization — a plausible source of residual GP-vs-XPlace gaps.

**Lead (strong):** for bookshelf (ISPD2005), sw_only infers the die box from the outermost FIXED-terminal
*coordinates* and **ignores the `.scl` core rows**:
- `DataBase::set_bookshelf_node_position` (DataBase.cpp:880-883): `m_max_x/m_max_y = max(terminal x,y)`.
- `bookshelf_end` (DataBase.cpp:905): `die = Box((0,0),(m_max_x,m_max_y))`.
- `add_bookshelf_row` (DataBase.cpp:871) is an **empty no-op** — the row-based die extent is never read.
XPlace defines the die from the core rows / aux die (`die_ll`,`die_ur`). Inferring from terminal LL
coords (no terminal sizes, no rows) is almost certainly why the die differs.
