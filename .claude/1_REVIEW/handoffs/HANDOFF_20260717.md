# Handoff 2026-07-17 — Preconditioner + MMS mixed-size

## Headline
**The preconditioner is essential for movable-macro (MMS) convergence.** The long-standing
"preconditioner is a wash" conclusion was an artifact of only ever testing on fixed-macro
benchmarks (ISPD2005 macros are FIXED terminals; ISPD2015 macros pre-placed). On MMS it's decisive.

## What happened this session
1. **λ-overshoot investigation (put on pin).** Traced the ISPD2005 λ gap: the forward `dct_normalize`
   1/N is a PURE cosmetic λ rescale (absorbed by the ratio-based λ_init + scale-invariant schedule);
   NOT the overshoot lever. The one non-invariant coupling is the preconditioner's
   `num_pins + pcoef·λ·area` sum. Measured on adaptec1: sw_only's λ runs a constant **~50× smaller**
   than XPlace (field-norm constant, NOT site_width — site_width cancels on ISPD2005), so `alpha_2`
   is ~50× under-weighted → precond stays pin-dominated. Added `precond_density_scale` knob +
   `precond_a1/a2_norm` trace cols. BUT basis-matching (scale=50) was a wash-to-worse on fixed-macro
   adaptec1 — because adaptec1 has NO movable macros.
2. **Benchmark landscape.** Confirmed we had NO movable-macro benchmark locally, and XPlace's paper
   only ran ISPD2005+2015 (both non-movable-macro). Acquired **MMS** (Modern Mixed-Size) from
   DREAMPlace's ungated Dropbox (`mms_mod.zip`, ~331MB) → `vck5000/host/benchmarks/mms/`
   (adaptec1-5, bigblue1-4, newblue1-7; bookshelf; macros movable). Parser verified.
3. **The payoff — MMS adaptec1 A/B/C** (seed 42, 512 grid):
   | precond | converged | iters | overflow | GP HPWL |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | OFF | NO (max 1200) | 1200 | 0.126 | 1.048e8 |
   | ON, scale=1 | YES | 612 | 0.038 | 6.363e7 |
   | ON, scale=50 | YES | 618 | 0.040 | 6.389e7 |
   scale=1 wins (macros self-damp via their own huge area term; global a2frac is a red herring).
4. **Viz:** movable macros now drawn RED (`Visualizer.cpp`, area > 0.02% die). Gifs of OFF (chaos,
   non-converged) and B/ON (clean convergence) generated; red-macro B gif regenerating at handoff time.

## Caveats (do not over-read)
- **6.36e7 is NOT comparable to fixed-macro XPlace 7.06e7** — different problem (macros movable),
  and it's **GP-only, no macro legalization** → macros may overlap → optimistically low HPWL.
- IO pads render OUTSIDE the die box — VERIFIED correct (peripheral ring ~4% outside core, faithful
  to the .pl; die box = core-row bbox). Not a bug.
